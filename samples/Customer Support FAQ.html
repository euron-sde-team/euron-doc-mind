Atlas Customer Support FAQ

Answers to the questions our support team hears most often. For urgent problems call the 24/7 line.

How do I reset an Atlas robot?

Press and hold the power button on the rear panel for 10 seconds until the status ring turns amber. Wait 30 seconds, then press the button once. The robot restarts, relocalises on the site map and reports back to Fleet within two minutes.

What does the warranty cover?

Atlas robots carry a 24-month warranty on parts and labour from the date of deployment. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 1,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Damage from forklifts, water or unapproved modifications is not covered.

How do firmware updates work?

Fleet software installs updates during the maintenance window your site sets, one robot at a time, so throughput never drops by more than 10 percent during an update.

How do I contact support?

  • Support portal: open a ticket at any time; P3 tickets are answered within one business day.
  • Phone: the 24/7 support line is for P1 incidents such as a stopped fleet.
  • Email: support@northwind.example for billing and account questions.

Can Atlas work in cold storage?

Atlas v1 is rated for 0 to 40 degrees Celsius. Atlas v2, available from March 2027, operates down to minus 10 degrees Celsius.
